Credit unions play a vital role in their communities, offering member-first service, personalized financial solutions, and relationships built on trust. But behind the scenes, many credit union finance teams are stretched thin, juggling increasing invoice volumes, regulatory demands, and time-consuming manual processes. That’s where accounts payable (AP) automation can help.
