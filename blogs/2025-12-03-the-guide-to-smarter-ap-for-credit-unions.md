Why It’s Time to Rethink AP in Credit Unions Credit unions are built on a foundation of trust, service, and community. Behind that member-first mission is a finance team working hard to keep everything running smoothly. But for many, the accounts payable (AP) process is stuck in the past: paper-based, time-consuming, and prone to error.
