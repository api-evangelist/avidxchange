One of the toughest parts of managing a community is keeping everyone aligned on the numbers. When invoices, approvals, and payments are scattered across paper files and inboxes, it’s easy for board members to feel in the dark—and for managers to get blindsided by questions they can’t quickly answer. Digital audit trails change that.
