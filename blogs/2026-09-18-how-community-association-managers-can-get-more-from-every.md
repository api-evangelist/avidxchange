Community association finance leaders are being asked to do more than simply process invoices and pay suppliers. They’re expected to control costs, improve financial visibility, strengthen supplier relationships, and support portfolio growth—all while managing the same lean teams. That’s why it’s time to stop thinking about payments as a back-office function.
