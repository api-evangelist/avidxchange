For finance leaders at multi-entity banks, managing accounts payable (AP) can be a constant juggling act. Each entity might operate under a different charter, serve a different customer segment, or use unique accounting structures. That can lead to inconsistent workflows, siloed data, and rising costs as teams work in parallel systems.
