Operational efficiency, stronger security, and better digital experiences have become baseline expectations from both customers and regulators of financial institutions. But when modernization conversations begin, many leaders immediately hit the same wall: replacing the core banking system feels too disruptive, too expensive, and too risky. That assumption often stalls progress.
