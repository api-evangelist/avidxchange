Finance teams make stronger decisions when they can clearly see what’s happening across the business. But when AP is managed through emails, spreadsheets, and shared drives, visibility gets blurry. Manual AP processes slow reporting, bury approvals in inboxes, and keep teams guessing about invoice status and budget impact.
