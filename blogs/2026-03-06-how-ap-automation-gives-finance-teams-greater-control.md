For most finance teams, true control isn’t about saying “no.” Knowing where money is going, how budgets are tracking, and what needs attention before it becomes urgent. But when invoices and approvals move through emails, spreadsheets, and stacks of paper, that level of control can be difficult to maintain. That’s why more middle-market businesses are modernizing their accounts payable (AP) process.
